## Explain from what the reader knows

When asked to explain, find the reason and rebuild the picture from what the user knows. Explain unfamiliar terms by what they do; do not repeat them or substitute an offer to remove the feature.

### Bad
I found no written reason. Should I stop building the Effects assembly?

### Good
Each camera sends its image into an Effects assembly, where art direction paints on it. That is why the assembly is created for every camera.
